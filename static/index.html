Why NearHi

Less scrolling.
More saying hi.

Most chat apps put the whole world between you and the next person. NearHi starts with the people around you, so a conversation with a stranger can still feel like it happens close to home.

No account. No download.

Open nearhi.in, allow your camera and location, pick a distance. That is the whole setup.

Not in the mood? Skip.

Skip to the next person or stop at any moment. No awkward goodbyes, no hard feelings.

Not recorded.

We don't record video or audio. An automatic safety screen checks a still image every few seconds and discards it. Nobody watches live, so treat it as a help, not a guarantee.

Free right now.

No account, no card, no paywall. Right now it costs you nothing.
